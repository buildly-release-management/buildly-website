How It All Connects
🔁
CollabHub feeds Forge
Builders in the community publish components to Forge Marketplace, earning revenue and building reputation.
⚡
Labs orchestrates everything
Buildly Labs is where product operations happen — it can reference Forge components, coordinate CollabHub teams, and track Deploy releases.
🏗️
Deploy runs what you build
Buildly Deploy provides shared infrastructure, lowering costs for indie builders while maintaining production-grade reliability.
🎙️
Stories and community amplify the ecosystem
FirstCityFoundry and StartupGrind PDX bring new founders and builders into the network — and give existing members visibility and voice.
