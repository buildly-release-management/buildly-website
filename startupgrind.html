About StartupGrind PDX

Where Portland
founders connect.

StartupGrind PDX is the Portland chapter of the global StartupGrind founder community. We bring together builders, founders, developers, and investors for real conversations about what it takes to build something meaningful.

As part of the Buildly ecosystem, StartupGrind PDX bridges local relationships with global tools. Builders who attend our events get access to the Buildly network — and vice versa.
